Safety guidelines & FAQs

How does the verified badge work?

Everyone signs up with a school or workplace email. Personal emails like Gmail are blocked. Drivers can also add their car details to show a vehicle badge.

What is not allowed?

Harassment, unsafe driving, no-shows without notice, fake profiles and anything illegal. Reports go to the admin queue, and repeat or serious problems lead to suspension.

What should I do before getting in?

Check the name and the car match what you see in the app, tell a friend your trip is starting, and turn on Share live ETA on the trip screen.

How do I report someone?

After a trip, use Report a problem on the Review page. You can also open a ticket here at any time.

Location sharing & privacy

This class demo does not collect real GPS. The live trip map is a simulation. Your commutes, messages and tickets are saved only in your own browser on this device, and nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data removes them.
